COMPRESS ARENA

A multiplayer game about data compression

HOW TO PLAY

1 MOVE around the arena and find colored data blocks
2 CLICK matching patterns (same text like AABB + AABB)
3 Matching 2+ blocks compresses them for points
4 Rare patterns = more points. Known patterns = bonus
5 Full energy bar? BLAST noise at others to disrupt them
6 Arena shrinks every 60s. Survive and score the most!
